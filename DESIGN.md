## Brand & Style

### Personality & Values
The brand identity centers on absolute transparency, fiduciary integrity, and singular accountability. Operating in the dense residential and industrial nexus of Chunui Station (Bucheon), the service relies on a sole principal licensed broker handling each client personally from intake to title transfer. The visual language rejects hyperbole, loud sales tropes, and gimmicky badges. Instead, it projects legal clarity, meticulous documentation, and quiet institutional confidence.

### Target Audience
1. **End-user Residents & Families:** First-time buyers, newlyweds, and tenants requiring rigorous contract protection, registry validation, and escrow clarity.
2. **Business Owners & Operators:** Light manufacturing, IT, logistics, and retail entrepreneurs seeking commercial facilities or knowledge industry centers (지식산업센터) with verified clearance heights, load capacities, and zoning permissions.
3. **Prudent Property Investors:** Individuals requiring objective yields calculated on prevailing tenancy metrics without speculative hype.

### Design Movement: Modern Fiduciary Minimalism
The aesthetic bridges contemporary corporate fintech clarity with the architectural precision of an official public registry:
- **Clean Structure:** Generous negative space paired with high-legibility tabular frameworks, verified process pipelines, and distinct badge indicators.
- **Subdued Premium Accents:** Deep authoritative navy anchors the platform, balanced by warm amber accents symbolizing value and vigilance, and clean slate neutral fields that mirror high-grade legal stationery.
- **Intentional Density:** Property attributes (floor-to-area ratios, maintenance fees, senior lien checks) are displayed in crisp, structured key-value modules rather than unstructured narrative blocks.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
- **Primary Navy (`#0F294A`):** The dominant authority color, symbolizing fiduciary responsibility, legal grounding, and institutional reliability. Applied to navigation headers, primary headline titles, active state anchors, and primary conversion buttons.
- **Secondary Warm Amber (`#D97706`):** An alert, grounded accent used sparingly for conversion highlights, verified broker actions, price highlights, and contract stage callouts.
- **Tertiary Modern Emerald (`#0D9488`):** Used for certified status tags, confirmed registry clearance badges, and positive verification steps (e.g., "등기부 확인 완료").
- **Neutral Slate (`#475569`):** A balanced, cool slate gray calibrated for long-form legal disclosures, property descriptions, and secondary metadata without visual fatigue.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Usage
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, clean architectural off-white that prevents high-contrast glare while maintaining crisp contrast against pure white cards.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Base layer for interactive units, tabular cards, and property sheets.
- **Border Hierarchy:** `#E2E8F0` for interior card dividers and layout borders; `#CBD5E1` for form inputs and structured table frames.
- **Typography Contrast:** Maximum contrast with `#0F172A` for primary headlines, `#475569` for body copy, and `#94A3B8` for meta timestamps and footnotes.

## Typography

### Font Selection & Pairing
To guarantee clean rendering across high-density Korean script while maintaining a sleek, modern UI, **Plus Jakarta Sans** is selected for primary digital hierarchy alongside system font fallbacks (`Pretendard`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Malgun Gothic`). This creates crisp alphanumeric balance (crucial for deposit amounts, square meter conversions, and room layouts) with dense, perfectly legible Korean letterforms.

### Typographic Discipline
- **Tight Headings:** High tracking compression (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) on titles to preserve structural solidarity and prevent Korean word breaks from trailing.
- **Comfortable Reading Metrics:** Body text maintains a 1.6x to 1.7x line height ratio to accommodate complex Sino-Korean legal vocabulary (e.g., 중개대상물 확인설명서, 근저당권 말소조건, 건축물대장 위반여부).
- **Tabular Numerals:** Price figures, square meters ($m^2$), and pyeong figures must render with uniform tabular figure width to preserve vertical column alignment in pricing tables.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Model & Container Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum container width of `1240px`, centered with `2rem` outer padding and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. Stacks data cards into sequential vertical units.

### Rhythm and Spacing Scale
- **`space-xs` (4px) / `space-sm` (8px):** Internal element spacing within status badges, icon-label pairs, and price subtext.
- **`space-md` (16px):** Standard internal padding for list elements, filter dropdowns, and compact data fields.
- **`space-lg` (24px):** Interior padding for property cards, consultation forms, and process cards.
- **`space-xl` (40px) / `space-2xl` (64px):** Separation between sub-modules, table groups, and feature tiers.
- **`space-3xl` (96px):** Macro section clearance between landing page blocks (Hero → Principle Pledge → Property Matrix → Process Flow → Broker Verification → Contact & Location).

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Structural Tonal Grounding
The design intentionally avoids floaty, game-like 3D shadows. Depth is established through crisp surface tonal layering, clean architectural hairline borders, and subtle diffuse ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat canvas for neutral page foundation.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Content Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a `1px` solid border in `#E2E8F0`. Provides flat structural stability without reliance on heavy cast shadows.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Active Elements):** Raised cards on user interaction receive a subtle tinted shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.04);`
   - Border changes smoothly to `#CBD5E1`.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Header & Modal Drawers):** 
   - Sticky navigation header uses semi-opaque white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`) backed by a `12px` frosted glass blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a sharp bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
   - Modals and urgent consultation forms apply `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry: Soft Architectural Precision
The design uses a restrained `Soft (1)` corner radius geometry. This preserves an authoritative, document-like presentation suitable for legal transactions, deeds, and official real estate disclosures.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Status badges, tag markers, form input borders, and small operational buttons.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Property listing cards, process timeline modules, testimonial panels, and consultation input sheets.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Hero callout banners, broker credential panels, and interactive map embed frames.
- **No Full Pills for Actions:** Main CTA buttons adopt the `0.375rem` (6px) or `0.5rem` (8px) standard radius rather than round pill forms to maintain formal gravitas.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Broker Consultation):** Deep navy background (`#0F294A`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), font weight 600, radius `0.5rem`. Subtle hover shift to `#1E3A8A` with a micro Y-axis translation of `-1px`.
- **Secondary Action (Map & Location / Call):** Clean white background with `#0F294A` text and `#CBD5E1` border. Hover changes to `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Accent Highlight (Urgent Reservation / Contact):** Warm Amber background (`#D97706`), white text, reserved for direct 1:1 phone calls and instant Naver reservation prompts.

### 2. Status Badges & Category Chips
- **Contract Type Chips:** Bordered compact tags (`0.25rem` radius, `0.75rem` font size).
  - *매매 (Sale):* Navy tint (`bg-[#0F294A]/10 text-[#0F294A]`).
  - *전세 (Jeonse):* Emerald tint (`bg-[#0D9488]/10 text-[#0D9488]`).
  - *월세 (Monthly):* Slate tint (`bg-[#475569]/10 text-[#334155]`).
- **Safety Indicator:** "등기부 대조 확인" badge with a small shield icon, soft green background (`#ECFDF5`), and emerald text (`#059669`).
- **Zero-False-Listing Seal:** "실매물 검증완료" in concise high-contrast label formatting.

### 3. Property & Information Cards
- **Card Shell:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`, internal padding `1.5rem`.
- **Image Module:** 16:10 aspect ratio container with subtle inner border overlay to preserve image frame edges.
- **Key-Value Matrix:** Clean tabular pairing for Floor Area ($m^2$/평), Floor tier, Maintenance fee, and Parking availability, using `#94A3B8` labels and `#0F172A` values.

### 4. Legal Compliance Tables
- Tables feature a structured header row in `#F8FAFC` with bold 12px uppercase slate labels.
- Borders use alternating subtle row dividers in `#F1F5F9`.
- Mandatory public disclosure block (Registration Number, Broker Bond 保障 금액, Address) enclosed in a bordered institutional document card with a faint `#F8FAFC` background.

### 5. Input Fields & Consultation Form
- Inputs feature a `0.375rem` radius, `0.875rem` typography, and `#E2E8F0` border.
- Active focus state transitions to `border-color: #0F294A` with a non-blur outline ring `2px solid rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.1)`.
- Helper text explicitly states non-disclosure and privacy guarantees under real estate broker regulations.

### 6. Five Broker Promises Grid
- 5 clean numbered modules representing the core broker commitments (Direct execution, written explanation, same-day registry renewal, statutory commission limits, zero false listings).
- Distinct serial index numerals in Warm Amber (`#D97706`), bold title, and concise 2-line explanation in Slate (`#475569`).
